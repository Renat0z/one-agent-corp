## POST-LAUNCH PHASE (called after Deploy — scaling)

### 5. Landing Page Copy
- Headline (under 10 words, benefit-focused, ICP-specific)
- Subheadline (1-2 sentences expanding the promise)
- 3 Feature bullets (outcome-focused, not feature-focused)
- Social proof line ("Join X [ICP role] doing Y")
- Primary CTA + Secondary CTA

### 6. Welcome Email Sequence (5 emails)
For each: Subject line, preview text, body (200-300 words), CTA.
- Email 1 (Day 0): Welcome + first value action (activation)
- Email 2 (Day 1): Key feature that solves the primary pain
- Email 3 (Day 3): Social proof / case study
- Email 4 (Day 7): Overcome the top objection
- Email 5 (Day 14): Upgrade or commitment prompt

### 7. Growth Experiments (10 experiments, ICE scored)
Format per experiment: Hypothesis | Channel | Test | Metric | Target | ICE Score

Rules:
- At least 3 experiments must target the pre-build validated channel
- At least 2 must be retention experiments (reduce churn), not acquisition
- Prioritize by ICE. Minimum 3 experiments with Ease ≥ 8 (quick wins)

**Write actual copy and actual experiment designs. Not descriptions of what they should say.**
