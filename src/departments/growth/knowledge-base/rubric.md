# Growth Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10. Apply the correct rubric based on phase.

---

## PRE-BUILD RUBRIC (Distribution Validation)

### Distribution Channel Specificity (0-4)
- 4: Single channel named with 30-day weekly plan, CAC estimate, and confidence rationale
- 3: Channel named with plan but missing cost estimate or confidence rationale
- 2: Channel named but plan is vague ("post on LinkedIn")
- 1: Multiple channels listed with no prioritization ("do SEO and cold outreach and Product Hunt")
- 0: No channel identified

### Growth Loop Completeness (0-3)
- 3: Full loop defined with conversion rate targets at each stage AND failure point identified
- 2: Loop defined but missing conversion targets or failure point
- 1: Loop described conceptually without measurable stages
- 0: No growth loop

### Pre-Sell Experiment Quality (0-3)
- 3: Specific test designed (format + ICP target + price + success signal + timeline ≤ 2 weeks)
- 2: Test designed but missing price or timeline
- 1: Pre-sell mentioned but not designed
- 0: No pre-sell experiment

**Pre-build pass threshold: 7/10**
**Fail signal: Distribution Channel Specificity = 0 or 1 → auto-fail regardless of total score**

---

## POST-LAUNCH RUBRIC (Scaling)

### Copy Clarity (0-3)
- 3: Headlines are ICP-specific, benefit-focused, would make the ICP stop scrolling
- 2: Good copy but generic — could apply to any SaaS
- 1: Feature-focused, not benefit-focused
- 0: No real copy written

### Conversion Elements (0-2)
- 2: All elements present — headline, subheadline, bullets, social proof, CTA, objection handling
- 1: Most elements present but missing 1-2
- 0: Incomplete

### Email Sequence Quality (0-2)
- 2: 5 emails with activation logic (email 1), retention logic (email 4-5), clear CTAs
- 1: Emails written but missing activation or retention logic
- 0: No sequence

### Experiment Quality (0-3)
- 3: 10 experiments with clear hypotheses, measurable metrics, ICE scores, ≥2 retention experiments
- 2: Experiments present but missing retention focus or ICE scores
- 1: Experiments listed without structure
- 0: No experiments

**Post-launch pass threshold: 7/10**
