# Offer Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### Offer Clarity (0-3)
- 3: Dream outcome is vivid, mechanism is unique, promise is specific and measurable
- 2: Good offer but mechanism or promise needs sharpening
- 1: Generic offer without differentiation
- 0: No clear offer

### Pricing Logic (0-2)
- 2: 3 tiers with clear ICP fit, logical feature gates, competitive positioning
- 1: Pricing exists but rationale unclear
- 0: No pricing

### Value Equation Application (0-3)
- 3: All 4 levers addressed: dream outcome maximized, likelihood increased, delay reduced, effort minimized
- 2: 2-3 levers addressed
- 1: Vague value claims
- 0: No value equation thinking

### Competitive Differentiation (0-2)
- 2: Positioning is unique, defensible, and compelling vs. alternatives
- 1: Positioning exists but similar to competitors
- 0: No differentiation

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
