# Operations Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### Financial Accuracy (0-3)
- 3: Unit economics use correct formulas, realistic numbers, clear assumptions
- 2: Most calculations correct with minor errors
- 1: Formulas present but numbers unrealistic
- 0: No financial model

### Compliance Thoroughness (0-3)
- 3: All applicable frameworks (GDPR/CCPA/PCI) covered with specific, actionable items
- 2: 2 frameworks covered
- 1: One framework, surface-level
- 0: No compliance work

### Actionability (0-2)
- 2: Recommendations are specific, quantified, with implementation steps
- 1: Recommendations are general
- 0: No actionable output

### Cost Realism (0-2)
- 2: Costs are realistic for a micro-SaaS, break-even is achievable
- 1: Costs present but unrealistic
- 0: No cost analysis

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
