# Content Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### SEO Quality (0-3)
- 3: Keyword targeting natural, search intent matched, proper H-tag hierarchy, meta optimized
- 2: Good SEO basics but some optimization gaps
- 1: Keyword mentioned but no real SEO structure
- 0: No SEO consideration

### Content Depth (0-3)
- 3: 1,500+ words, comprehensive coverage, original insights, actionable advice
- 2: 1,000-1,499 words, mostly complete
- 1: Under 1,000 words or thin content
- 0: No real article written

### Documentation Clarity (0-2)
- 2: Clear structure, progressive disclosure, addresses real user questions
- 1: Outline exists but sparse
- 0: No documentation

### Calendar Viability (0-2)
- 2: Realistic 4-week calendar with diverse content types and distribution channels
- 1: Calendar exists but lacks variety
- 0: No content calendar

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
