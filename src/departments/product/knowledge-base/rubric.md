# Product Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### PRD Completeness (0-3)
- 3: Problem, users, solution, metrics, non-goals all present and specific
- 2: Most sections present but some missing detail
- 1: Incomplete PRD
- 0: No PRD

### User Story Quality (0-3)
- 3: 8+ stories in Gherkin format with clear acceptance criteria
- 2: Stories present but missing acceptance criteria
- 1: Stories too vague or too few
- 0: No user stories

### Roadmap Feasibility (0-2)
- 2: Roadmap is realistic, prioritized by ICE, maps to business goals
- 1: Roadmap exists but prioritization unclear
- 0: No roadmap

### Metric Quality (0-2)
- 2: 3+ specific, measurable KPIs with numeric targets
- 1: Vague metrics
- 0: No metrics

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
