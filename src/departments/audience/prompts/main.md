## Deliverables

### 1. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Demographics: role, company size, industry, geography
- Psychographics: goals, fears, aspirations
- Technographics: tools they use, tech sophistication
- Behavioral: how they buy, where they hang out, content they consume
- Trigger events: what makes them search for a solution NOW

### 2. List Building Strategy (5 channels)
For each channel:
- Channel name + specific tactic
- Estimated reach
- Time to first 100 leads
- Cost (free/paid)
- Implementation steps
