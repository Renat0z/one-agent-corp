# Audience Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### ICP Specificity (0-3)
- 3: Detailed profile with demographics, psychographics, technographics, trigger events
- 2: Good ICP but missing some dimensions
- 1: Vague ICP
- 0: No ICP

### Channel Viability (0-3)
- 3: 5 channels with specific tactics, realistic estimates, and clear implementation steps
- 2: 3-4 channels with partial detail
- 1: Channels listed without strategy
- 0: No channels

### Template Quality (0-2)
- 2: Templates are personalized, value-focused, with clear CTAs — would get responses
- 1: Generic templates
- 0: No templates

### JV/Win-Back Quality (0-2)
- 2: JV framework is actionable, win-back emails are compelling
- 1: Framework exists but generic
- 0: No JV or win-back content

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
