# Competitive Intelligence Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10.

## Scoring Dimensions

### Analysis Depth (0-3)
- 3: Five Forces fully analyzed with specific evidence, not just categorization
- 2: Most forces analyzed with some depth
- 1: Surface-level analysis
- 0: No real analysis

### Positioning Clarity (0-2)
- 2: Clear positioning map with identified white space and actionable differentiation
- 1: Positioning mentioned but unclear strategy
- 0: No positioning work

### Moat Defensibility (0-3)
- 3: 2-3 realistic moats identified with specific development plan
- 2: Moats identified but development path unclear
- 1: Vague moat claims
- 0: No moat analysis

### Battle Card Quality (0-2)
- 2: 3 competitors with specific strengths/weaknesses, objection handlers, proof points
- 1: Generic competitor comparison
- 0: No battle cards

**Pass threshold: 7/10**
