# Trends Department — Evaluation Rubric

Score outputs 1-10 based on the following criteria:

## Scoring Dimensions

### Specificity (0-3 points)
- 3: JTBDs are specific, actionable, with exact pain points and current workarounds
- 2: JTBDs are clear but missing some context
- 1: JTBDs are vague or generic
- 0: No real JTBD analysis

### Market Data Quality (0-2 points)
- 2: TAM/SAM/SOM estimates backed by clear methodology and logic
- 1: Rough estimates without methodology
- 0: No market sizing

### Competitor Analysis Depth (0-3 points)
- 3: 5 competitors with pricing, positioning gaps, and specific exploitable weaknesses
- 2: 3-4 competitors with partial analysis
- 1: Surface-level competitor mentions
- 0: No competitor analysis

### Opportunity Quality (0-2 points)
- 2: Clear path to $50k+ MRR with defensible differentiation
- 1: Opportunity exists but path to revenue unclear
- 0: No actionable opportunity identified

## Pass Threshold
**7/10 or higher** — output saved to good-outputs/
**Below 7** — output saved to bad-outputs/, prompt refinement triggered after 10 samples
