# ICP Definition — profitbridge-ai-intel

## ICP DEFINITION

### Who (named, specific)
- Role/Title: The "Overwhelmed Founder-Operator" (CEO/Owner)
- Company type: Performance Marketing Agencies & E-commerce Brands (7–25 employees, $1M–$5M ARR)
- Geography: North America (US/Canada) and UK

### Primary Pain (the ONE problem they pay to solve TODAY)
> "I know I'm making money, but I have no idea why my bank balance doesn't match my dashboard, and I'm terrified to scale spend because I can't see my true net margin in real-time."
- Pain intensity (1-10): 9
- How often they feel this pain: Daily (every time they check ad spend vs. bank balance)
- What it costs them (time or money): $4,000–$10,000/month in "invisible leaks" (untracked software subs, payment processing fees, and inefficient ad-spend-to-profit ratios).

### Current Solution (what they use NOW)
- Tool/method: Frankenstein Spreadsheet (Google Sheets + Zapier + manual export from QuickBooks)
- Why it's failing them: It’s always 48 hours out of date, formulas break when they add new channels, and it requires 5+ hours of manual data entry per week.
- Monthly spend on current solution: $0 (software) + $1,500 (pro-rated cost of Founder's or Operations Manager's time).

### Willingness to Pay
- Expected monthly budget for a solution: $300–$600
- Price sensitivity: Medium — They view this as an "insurance policy" against scaling into a loss; if it saves 1% of margin, it pays for itself 10x over.

### Where They Hang Out
- Communities: Limited Society (7-figure agency group), AdWorld Conference attendees, r/ecommerce, "Agency Operators" Slack.
- Tools they use daily: Slack, Triple Whale/Northbeam, QuickBooks Online, Shopify Admin, Google Ads.
- Events/conferences: SaaSNorth, Affiliate Summit East/West.

### ICP Validation Score (1-10): 9
Justification: This ICP has high cash flow but low visibility, making the "Profit Guard" value proposition an immediate ROI-driven sell rather than a "nice-to-have" luxury.
