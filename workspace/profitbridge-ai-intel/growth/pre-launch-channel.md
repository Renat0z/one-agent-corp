# Pre-launch Distribution — profitbridge-ai-intel

## PRE-LAUNCH DISTRIBUTION PLAN

### Primary Channel (ONE channel only — the first 10 customers)
- Channel: Cold Email (High-Personalization)
- Why this channel for this ICP: Founder-Operators of $1M-$5M agencies are protective of their time and rarely "hang out" on social media for business tools; however, they are obsessively tied to their inbox for financial and operational alerts.
- Specific targeting: Apollo.io filter: Job Title (CEO, Founder, Owner) AND Industry (Advertising & Services, E-commerce) AND Headcount (11-50) AND Location (US, UK, Canada) AND Technology Used (Shopify, Meta Ads, Google Ads).
- Weekly execution: 150 highly-personalized emails/week (30/day) using liquid syntax for specific brand mentions.
- Estimated conversion rate: 7% (Lead-to-Meeting) -> 20% (Meeting-to-Pre-order) = 1.4% overall.
- Time to first 10 customers: 6 weeks.
- CAC from this channel: $150 (Cost of Apollo/Instantly subscription + 10 hours/week founder time).
