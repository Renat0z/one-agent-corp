# Offer Design — profitbridge-ai-intel

## OFFER DESIGN

### Core Value Proposition
> "For Overwhelmed Founder-Operators of 7-figure agencies and e-com brands, ProfitBridge AI is the Financial Command Center that plugs 'invisible' margin leaks in real-time, unlike manual spreadsheets or complex ERPs that only show the past."

### Pricing Model
- Model: Monthly Subscription (SaaS)
- Tiers:
  | Tier | Price/mo | What's included | Target customer |
  |------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
  | Starter | $299 | 3 Data Integrations, Real-time Margin Tracking, Slack Alerts | Solo/Lean Agencies |
  | Pro | $599 | Unlimited Integrations, Predictive Scaling Simulators, Custom Dashboards | Growing teams ($1M+ ARR) |
  | Scale | $999 | White-label Reporting, Multi-entity Support, Dedicated Success Manager | Large Agencies ($3M+ ARR) |

### Unit Economics
- Target ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): $550/month
- Estimated avg customer lifetime: 24 months
- LTV = ARPU × lifetime: $13,200
- Estimated CAC (acquisition cost): $2,200
- LTV/CAC ratio: 6.0x
- Payback period: 4 months

### Value Stacks (Hormozi style)
What do they get beyond the core product?
1. **The "Margin Leak" Audit** — One-time deep dive to find $2k+ in immediate savings. Value: $1,500
2. **Proprietary Ad-Spend Predictor** — AI model to forecast ROAS vs. Net Profit. Value: $300/mo
3. **The "Sleep Well" Guarantee** — 30-day "Find your ROI or it's free" + double your money back on the first month's fee.

### Competitive Positioning
- Cheapest competitor: $49/month (Generic Bookkeeping Apps)
- Our price vs cheapest: 510% Premium
- Why our price is justified: We don't just "track" expenses; we provide the decision-engine that identifies exactly where the founder is losing $5k-$10k/month in real-time.

### Offer Validation Score (1-10): 9.2
Does LTV/CAC >= 3? YES | Payback < 12 months? YES
