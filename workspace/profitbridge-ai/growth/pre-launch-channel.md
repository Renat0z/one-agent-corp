# Pre-launch Distribution — profitbridge-ai

## PRE-LAUNCH DISTRIBUTION PLAN

### Primary Channel (ONE channel only — the first 10 customers)
- Channel: Cold Email (Hyper-personalized Outreach)
- Why this channel for this ICP: Shopify operators are bombarded with generic ads but respond to data-backed audits of their specific store leaks. High-volume stores (500+ SKUs) are easily identifiable via StoreLeads or BuiltWith, allowing for surgical targeting of those running Google Ads.
- Specific targeting: Shopify Stores > $30k Revenue > Running Google Ads > Fashion/Home Decor niches > Contact: Founder or Head of Ops.
- Weekly execution: 150 highly personalized emails/week (30 per day).
- Estimated conversion rate: 7% (Demo book rate) -> 20% (Close rate) = 1.4% total.
- Time to first 10 customers: 6 weeks.
- CAC from this channel: $150 (Apollo.io/Instantly subscription + manual research time).

### Pre-launch Checklist (do BEFORE writing code)
- [ ] [Scrape list of 500 Shopify stores in Fashion/Home Decor with >500 SKUs using StoreLeads]
- [ ] [Run "Ghost Spend Audit" on 10 stores to find OOS items still being advertised to use as cold email social proof]
- [ ] [Join 'Shopify Entrepreneurs' and 'Google Ads for eCommerce' Facebook/Slack groups to monitor "ROAS vs Profit" complaints]
- [ ] [Set up a Carrd landing page with a "Profit Leak Calculator" to capture emails for the waitlist]

### Launch Day Actions
- [ ] Product Hunt listing targeting the "E-commerce Operations" category.
- [ ] LinkedIn "Build in Public" post showing the $ amount saved for the first 3 beta testers.
- [ ] 50 direct outreach messages to the highest-intent leads from the scraped "Ghost Spend" list.

### Growth Loop (post-launch)
How does one user bring another?
> "Profit Recovery Reports": Every month, the app generates a branded "Waste Prevented" PDF. When the user shares this to LinkedIn or Slack groups to brag about their optimized margins, it includes a referral link for other operators to "Audit their Waste."

### Channel Validation Score (1-10): 9
Is there evidence this ICP uses this channel? YES — High-volume Shopify founders are notoriously active in cold outbound ecosystems (buying apps/services) and are easily indexed by e-commerce intelligence tools like StoreLeads.
