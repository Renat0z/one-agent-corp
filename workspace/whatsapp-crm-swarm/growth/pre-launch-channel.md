# Pre-launch Distribution — whatsapp-crm-swarm

## PRE-LAUNCH DISTRIBUTION PLAN

### Primary Channel (ONE channel only — the first 10 customers)
- Channel: Direct Instagram Outreach (Focus on Aesthetic Clinic Owners/Managers)
- Why this channel for this ICP: In Brazil, aesthetic clinics live on Instagram; it is their primary storefront and where owners are most active monitoring their own tags and DMs.
- Specific targeting: Profiles of "Clínicas de Estética" in SP/RJ/BH with 10k-50k followers and active "Agende aqui" WhatsApp links in bio.
- Weekly execution: 100 personalized video/audio DMs per week to owners/managers.
- Estimated conversion rate: 10% (Lead to Demo) -> 20% (Demo to Close) = 2% Total.
- Time to first 10 customers: 5 weeks.
- CAC from this channel: $0 (Direct manual labor/Time cost).

### Pre-launch Checklist (do BEFORE writing code)
- [ ] Manual Audit: Message 20 clinics as a "mystery shopper" to document their current response time and lack of funnel follow-up.
- [ ] Solution Interview: Present the "Unread Leads = Lost Revenue" dashboard mockup to 5 clinic owners to validate the R$ 597 price point.
- [ ] Community Infiltration: Join 3 Facebook/WhatsApp groups of "Gestores de Clínicas de Estética" to monitor common complaints about "vendedores que esquecem de responder".
- [ ] Landing Page: Create a 1-page "Stop Lead Leakage" waitlist highlighting the Evolution API + Kanban integration.

### Launch Day Actions
- [ ] Post "The Aesthetic Clinic Revenue Leak" case study on LinkedIn and Instagram tagging early validators.
- [ ] Instagram Story "Behind the Scenes" showing the Kanban board moving a R$ 5k procedure lead from 'New' to 'Paid'.
- [ ] 50 direct outreach messages to the "warm" list of owners contacted during the validation phase.

### Growth Loop (post-launch)
How does one user bring another?
> "Powered by Swarm" branding on the automated WhatsApp greeting/follow-up messages sent to the clinic's patients, triggering curiosity in other service-based business owners who receive the message. An "Affiliate Dashboard" inside the CRM rewards clinic owners with 1 free month for every other clinic they refer.

### Channel Validation Score (1-10): 9
Is there evidence this ICP uses this channel? YES — 95% of Brazilian aesthetic clinics use Instagram as their #1 lead generation source and the owners personally manage or closely monitor the account's performance.
