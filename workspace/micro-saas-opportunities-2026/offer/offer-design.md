# Offer Design — micro-saas-opportunities-2026

## OFFER DESIGN

### Core Value Proposition
> "For High-Ticket Agency Owners burning ad spend on no-shows, **ShowUp Flow** is the 'Psychological Commitment' engine that recovers 30% of lost bookings through automated VSL-consumption tracking and friction-based qualification, unlike generic schedulers that let leads ghost for free."

### Pricing Model
- Model: Monthly Subscription + Performance-based "Recovery" Credits
- Tiers:
  | Tier | Price/mo | What's included | Target customer |
  |------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
  | Starter | $97 | Up to 50 bookings/mo, VSL Tracking, Basic SMS | Solo Consultants |
  | Pro | $197 | 200 bookings/mo, "Staking" Deposit Logic, CRM Sync | Boutique Agencies |
  | Scale | $497 | Unlimited bookings, Custom White-label, API access | High-Volume Teams |

### Unit Economics
- Target ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): $165/month
- Estimated avg customer lifetime: 14 months
- LTV = ARPU × lifetime: $2,310
- Estimated CAC (acquisition cost): $450 (Cold Email + Paid Meta Ops)
- LTV/CAC ratio: 5.13x
- Payback period: 2.7 months

### Value Stacks (Hormozi style)
What do they get beyond the core product?
1. **The "Ghost-Buster" SMS Sequence** — value: $497 (Proven templates used in $1M+ agencies)
2. **VSL Engagement Dashboard** — value: $97/mo (See exactly where leads drop off before the call)
3. **The "Show-Up" Guarantee** — If your show-up rate doesn't increase by at least 15% in 30 days, we refund the sub and pay for your next 50 bookings' SMS costs.

### Competitive Positioning
- Cheapest competitor: $29/month (Calendly/Acuity)
- Our price vs cheapest: Premium at 330% more (Starter)
- Why our price is justified: We aren't a "calendar"; we are a revenue recovery tool—one single saved $5k sale pays for 2 years of the software.

### Offer Validation Score (1-10): 9.2
Does LTV/CAC >= 3? YES | Payback < 12 months? YES
