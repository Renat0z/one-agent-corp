# Pre-launch Distribution — micro-saas-opportunities-2026

## PRE-LAUNCH DISTRIBUTION PLAN

### Primary Channel (ONE channel only — the first 10 customers)
- Channel: LinkedIn Cold Outreach (Automated + Manual Personalization)
- Why this channel for this ICP: High-Ticket Agency Owners (Performance/Lead Gen) are hyper-active on LinkedIn for their own lead generation and authority building; their profiles explicitly list "Agency Owner" and "Performance Marketing."
- Specific targeting: LinkedIn Sales Navigator Filter: Job Title: "Founder" OR "Owner" AND Industry: "Advertising Services" OR "Marketing Services" AND Company Size: 2-10 AND Keywords: "High Ticket" OR "Lead Gen."
- Weekly execution: 250 connection requests/week + 100 personalized InMails to those who accept.
- Estimated conversion rate: 4% (Lead to Booking) -> 20% (Booking to Close for Beta) = 0.8% overall.
- Time to first 10 customers: 4 weeks (assuming 1,000 outreaches total).
- CAC from this channel: $150 (Cost of Sales Navigator + Automation Tool like HeyReach/Expandi + Time).

### Pre-launch Checklist (do BEFORE writing code)
- [ ] Search LinkedIn for "Agency Owner" + "High Ticket" to verify a pool of at least 5,000+ prospects exists.
- [ ] Run a "Problem Validation" poll or post in the "7-Figure Agency" Facebook Group to confirm no-show rates are a top 3 pain point.
- [ ] Reach out to 10 Agency Owners offering a "No-Show Audit" to see their current CRM/VSL drop-off data.
- [ ] Create a Carrd landing page with a "VSL-Consumption Tracking" demo video and a "Join Beta" waitlist.

### Launch Day Actions
- [ ] Product Hunt listing focusing on "The No-Show Killer for Agencies."
- [ ] "The Math of Ghosting" Long-form LinkedIn post tagging 10 agency owners who participated in validation.
- [ ] 50 direct outreach messages to the "Warm Waitlist" collected during the pre-sell phase.

### Growth Loop (post-launch)
How does one user bring another?
> "Powered by ShowUp Flow" badge on the booking confirmation page shown to the agency's leads (many of whom are also business owners/entrepreneurs). Referral program: Agencies get 1 month free for every other agency owner they onboard via a unique "Consultant Partner" link.
