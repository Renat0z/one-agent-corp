### Who (named, specific)
- **Role/Title**: High-Ticket Agency Owner (Performance Marketing or Lead Gen)

- **Geography**: United States & United Kingdom (Tier 1 English-speaking markets)

### Primary Pain (the ONE problem they pay to solve TODAY)
> "I’m burning thousands on Facebook Ads to get bookings, but half the people don't show up, and the ones who do haven't even watched the VSL. My closers are frustrated and my CPL is effectively doubled because of the ghosting."
- **Pain intensity (1-10)**: 9

- **What it costs them (time or money)**: $2,000–$5,000/month in wasted ad spend and $10k+ in lost commissions/revenue.

### Current Solution (what they use NOW)
- **Tool/method**: Calendly + Basic Zapier integration to GoHighLevel or ActiveCampaign.
- **Why it's failing them**: Generic reminders aren't "indoctrinating" the lead. It lacks built-in psychological friction (homework/deposits) to filter out window shoppers before the call.
- **Monthly spend on current solution**: $15 (Calendly) + $297 (GHL) + $50 (Zapier).

### Willingness to Pay
- **Expected monthly budget for a solution**: $99–$199
- **Price sensitivity**: Low — If the tool saves just *one* $3k sale per month, the ROI is 15x-30x.

### Where They Hang Out

- **Tools they use daily**: Meta Ads Manager, Slack, GoHighLevel, Stripe, Loom.
- **Events/conferences**: Funnel Hacking Live, Traffic & Conversion Summit.

### ICP Validation Score (1-10): 9
